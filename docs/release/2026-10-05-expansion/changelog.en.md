October 5: New regions, habitat trails and themed expeditions

Explore eight new regions: Twin Islands, Ghost Tower, Safari Fields, Magnetic Mine, Volcanic Ruins, Dragon Valley, Abandoned Lab and Silver Snow Ridge. Badges, the Champion and regional research unlock them gradually. The original four routes remain, with the same first 151 Pokémon.

The eight regions now have 24 distinct habitat trails with different partner pools. Choices show example partners and actual level bands, and tracking portraits are restored in the route list. New-region levels are sampled from the regional band instead of following your leader; deep exploration uses the upper half. Existing encounters cannot be rerolled by switching partners or restarting. Original routes still scale to your partner.

Each region has rotating targets and independent research. Clues are shared across a region; tracked or guaranteed encounters may use another trail. Discover five local species to unlock deeper investigation. Rare partners are more common in the new pools; after five random discoveries below four stars, the next random discovery guarantees at least four stars. Legendary progression requirements still apply.

Eight themed dungeons and a Champion Expedition take 1–3 of your own partners through battles, upgrades and camps. Themes have distinct bosses, first-clear rewards and a harder mode. Enemy scaling is set on entry and does not chase level-ups during the run. Themes share an eight-node structure; unique map puzzles and long storylines are not included.

After clearing a new theme, choose a direction to find a capturable partner rated at least four stars. After four such clears without a five-star partner, the fifth guarantees one. Shiny odds are 1/16 for dungeon partners and 1/48 for exploration. First clears reward evolution items and Ultra Balls; repeat clears can also yield theme items.

If the encounter list or an item stack is full, new-theme rewards are held for collection. Collect them in the dungeon lobby, or discard them after a second confirmation, before starting another run. Regional research links discoveries, captures, tracking and a local clear with a one-time growth reward.

Saves now use V18. V5–V17 saves migrate to V18; V18 can be exported and imported again. Existing Forest dungeon progress is retained. Keep original backups unchanged; V18 cannot be imported into old firmware.

Back up first: https://luobata.github.io/ESP32-PokemonGo/
Existing compatible devices can use PokeWalk-update.zip at https://github.com/Luobata/ESP32-PokemonGo/releases/latest and follow its USB instructions. Full community installation overwrites the old save area: back up before installation and import afterwards.
The new content requires a firmware update. The online/offline save tools fixed on October 5 already support V18; older tools must be refreshed or downloaded again.
