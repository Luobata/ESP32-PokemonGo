Start and controls
Meet Professor Oak, then choose Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle or Pikachu. Existing saves resume. Offline play needs no network setup. A selects the previous item, B the next and C confirms. Hold B to return; hold C to turn off the screen. After 60 idle seconds the screen turns off. The first press wakes it without activating an action. Home offers Care, Menu and Encounters. Battles automatically choose learned, enabled moves without PP.

Radar
Menu > Radar uses nearby Wi-Fi without joining networks. A/B selects Track or View Encounters; C confirms. Tracking costs five stamina, adds one partner and starts a 120-second cooldown. The environment selects the pool. No signals means no tracking. Dots are not GPS, direction or distance.

Map roaming
After starting Johto, Menu > Roam offers the park at two Johto badges, ruins at three and Burned Tower at four. A/B selects a destination, C walks there, and C again interacts. Read the sign or NPC and observe two other traces to reach 3/3, then search at the fourth destination. Walking and observation are free. Successful searches cost five stamina, start that map's independent 180-second cooldown and reset its three observations. The final two destinations link to existing activities or routes.
Successful map searches advance route research and carried-egg exploration. Observations alone do not advance hatching or legendary evidence. Cooldowns use powered-on time, do not decrease while off, and survive reopening or restarting. With five encounters listed, both modes ask you to make room without replacing old partners. Failed searches do not charge stamina.

Shiny partners and chains
Base odds are 1/32 (3.125%) for radar and 1/24 (about 4.17%) for roaming, independent of species rarity. Species selection remains rarity-weighted. Both add the exploration-chain bonus: with w wins and base denominator d, chance is approximately 1/d + (1−1/d)×w/(w+2304). Discovery alone adds no win. Exploration battle victories grow the chain; confirming capture or losing an exploration battle ends it. Capture has a second confirmation. Leaving, changing maps and restarting preserve the chain; generated partners are not rerolled.
Pools, levels and probability tables: https://luobata.github.io/ESP32-PokemonGo/guide/#field

Care, exploration and growth
Feed uses berries even at low stamina; Play needs five stamina. Care benefits improve experience, discoveries and capture timing. Stamina caps at 100 and refills in about an hour. Ordinary exploration and route trainer practice cost five; Gym/rematch/Red battles ten; an Elite Four/Champion run twenty. Exploration branches change candidate pools and respect local level limits. In capture, A/B changes balls and C throws at the right moment. Failure allows a counterattack; choosing Battle commits to the fight. At evolution requirements, put the partner first and confirm in Care > Evolve.

Johto, activities and challenges
With any four Kanto badges, open Menu > Johto. Existing players retain qualification. Read the invitation, choose a Johto starter and follow the Adventure Journal. C advances dialogue, A/B chooses, and holding B pauses for later; replaying does not repeat rewards. Legendary fieldwork asks for a route branch and suitable living party member. Correct actions cost five stamina; hints and wrong choices are free. Optional solutions are collapsed in the guide.
The first Johto badge grants Togepi's egg; carry it through 12 valid Johto explorations, then hatch. At two badges, bring one partner and spend ten stamina on a six-point bug survey. You have THREE throws for the whole run, using your own balls. A/B selects an action; on Throw, press C when the pointer is blue. No throws or balls means throwing is unavailable. Compare catches and keep one. All six observations grant story evidence without a catch. Goldenrod research at six and twelve visits unlocks rescues of unchosen starters.
Unown walls start at three badges. Ice, whirl and tower expeditions open at five, six and seven badges: bring three partners and pay twenty stamina. HP/status persist; relay points let you bank rewards or continue. Whirl depths grant the Silver Wing. The five-badge Lake of Rage story awards a guaranteed shiny red Gyarados; cancelled/full-storage claims remain available. After Red, Johto Gyms automatically gain champion teams and separate achievements. The Journal shows missing conditions for Elm's final trial.

Party, storage and moves
Six party slots plus 753 warehouse slots preserve each individual, including duplicate species. Open Moves in party or warehouse details: A/B selects, C toggles, hold B returns. Keep at least one enabled move; newly learned moves start enabled. Settings follow the individual and are read-only during battles/expeditions.
Release directly from warehouse details. Confirmation defaults to Cancel and shows name, level and shininess. Release is irreversible; save failure retains the partner. Hold A in storage for filters and sorting. During battles/expeditions releasing is unavailable.
Full storage after capture requires selecting a warehouse replacement and confirming again; hold A changes sorting. Holding B can abandon the new capture after confirmation. Existing partners are never automatically replaced. Keep power on until the pending capture is stored.

Options and Wi-Fi time sync
Menu > Options controls translation, mute, volume, brightness and battery display. C edits volume/brightness, A increases, B decreases, C finishes; brightness is 10–100%. Settings persist. Raising volume does not unmute. Battery appears near names in Menu/Care; low battery is red.
For time sync, open Options > Wi-Fi Time and press C. Connect your phone to the PW- hotspot and password shown on the device; stay connected despite the no-internet warning. Open http://192.168.4.1, enter an available 2.4 GHz network or another device's hotspot credentials, then Connect and Save. Confirm Time Synchronized on the device. Setup closes after about five minutes; C toggles it, hold B exits, A outside setup retries sync. Saved networks reconnect at boot. First sync establishes a reference; later boot sync credits powered-off stamina. Offline play remains available.

Backup, import and updating
Desktop Chrome/Edge: https://luobata.github.io/ESP32-PokemonGo/. Choose a private backup folder, connect USB, authorize the serial port and close competing serial tools. On the device open Menu > Options > Back Up Save and press C. Wait for file write/readback and completion. Saves stay on your computer.
To restore, open Options > Import Save, select a .pksave on the website and request import. The device defaults to Cancel; select overwrite and confirm. Current progress is backed up first. After restart, reconnect and verify the receipt, partners, levels and Pokédex. Same-device V5–V32 backups are supported; V5–V31 migrate without editing files. New saves cannot go into unsupported older firmware. Developer restore.py does not restore expanded storage; use the website.
New gameplay requires firmware updating. Partitions and the backup container are unchanged from the previous Johto release. Refresh the website for the guide; replace older offline tools when needed. Back up first. Full community installation may erase progress, requiring restoration afterwards. Existing installations should prefer PokeWalk-update.zip from https://github.com/Luobata/ESP32-PokemonGo/releases/latest over USB, following its README. It checks layout and backs up before writing. If export fails, retain existing backups and use the protected update package instead of full installation over your only progress.
Offline: download the ZIP, unzip, run python3 server.py (Windows: py -3 server.py) with Python 3.8+, then open http://localhost:8767/. Keep original backups and errors on failure.
