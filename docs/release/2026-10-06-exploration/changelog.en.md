October 6: Rotating encounters, care bonuses and shiny chains

Each trail in the eight expanded regions now has two rotating visitors. Each region changes its visitor group every 12 explorations. The trail screen shows the current visitors; story gates, rarity rules and pity still apply.

Exploration can yield extra supplies, partner training or shiny traces. Mood raises the special-event chance from 12% to 32%. Training grants extra experience; shiny traces add an independent shiny roll. Care now gives positive bonuses: satiety, mood and intimacy provide 100–155% XP, while intimacy can expand the capture window by up to an extra 50%, within existing limits. Care shows the benefits. No daily tasks were added.

Exploration wins now build a persistent shiny chain for future encounters. Only wild battles from map exploration and badge activities count. Changing areas, leaving a screen and restarting preserve it; trainer, Gym and dungeon results are separate. Losing an exploration battle or confirming capture breaks the chain. Capture asks for confirmation first; cancelling costs no ball and preserves the chain. Escaping neither adds nor resets it.

Wild-win drops remain 70% balls, with higher-rarity opponents giving better balls and evolution items more often. Food supplies and fallback rewards for full stacks improve. Normal and deep clues both give supplies, and trainer matches provide berries and milk. Four-star frequency and pity were rebalanced. Map screens show actual wild level bands; expanded regions have fixed minimum levels and harder deep areas instead of scaling down to the leader.

Menu and Care show cumulative screen-on playtime across days and restarts; time not recorded by older firmware cannot be reconstructed. A new game guide covers all 151 partners, routes, items, evolution, dungeons, achievements, shinies and care calculators, with links to save tools:
https://luobata.github.io/ESP32-PokemonGo/guide/

Compared with the last public community revision, this also includes eight regions, 24 habitat trails, eight regional dungeons and a Champion Expedition. Badges, the Champion and research unlock them. Regions have tracking, research and deep exploration. Take 1–3 of your own partners into themed dungeons for first-clear/harder-mode rewards and capturable rare partners. New-theme rewards wait if inventories are full. The roster remains the first 151; dungeon themes share an eight-node structure.

Saves use V20. V5–V19 migrate, V20 round-trips, and older dungeon progress is retained. New chain/playtime fields receive version-specific defaults. Keep original backups unchanged; V20 cannot be imported into older firmware that does not understand it.

Update firmware for the new gameplay. Back up first at https://luobata.github.io/ESP32-PokemonGo/. Existing devices with matching layouts can use PokeWalk-update.zip from GitHub Releases over USB. Full community installation overwrites the old save area: back up before installing, then restore. Refresh the guide online or download a fresh offline ZIP; retain your original backups.
