Turn AI Passport into a pocket Pokémon adventure. Meet Professor Oak, choose a starter or Pikachu, and explore four areas for the first 151 Pokémon. Catch shinies, raise a party of six and earn evolution items through achievements. Train against twelve route opponents, then challenge Gyms, the Elite Four, the Champion and Red, or take your own partners into the Forest dungeon.

Battles automatically choose from learned moves, with no PP or forgetting. Pixel animation, scene music, volume, mute and screen sleep are included. Movement is inferred from Wi-Fi changes, not step counting. There are 191 moves; this is not a complete Gold/Silver recreation. This unofficial fan project credits artwork, music and naming sources in the repository.

Each badge unlocks another exploration activity. Rotating tracking targets, shinies and evolution items give you more to collect. Take your own partners through the Forest dungeon, combine upgrades and earn rewards along the way, and discover a capturable partner after clearing the final boss.

Menu and Care can show the battery level. Options includes a battery-display toggle, brightness, volume, mute and Wi-Fi time sync for powered-off stamina recovery. Online and offline USB tools back up saves and import them after device confirmation. Import requires the same device and matching firmware; keep your backup and matching installer before updating.
