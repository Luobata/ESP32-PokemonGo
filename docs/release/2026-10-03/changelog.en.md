October 3 update: Battery display, shiny encounters and dungeon partners

1. The Menu and Care pages now show a battery icon and percentage beside your partner’s name, using normal-sized text away from the screen edge. At 20% or less, the indicator turns red. Other pages and battle animations keep the full game view.

2. Added Menu > Options > Battery Display. Press C to toggle it. It is on by default and your choice survives restart.

3. Fixed achievements becoming unclaimable when reward items are full. You can now complete and claim the achievement; items fill only to their holding limit, with a message explaining any excess. No selling or discarding system was added.

4. Increased shiny odds by encounter source: passive encounters 1/64 (about 1.56%), active exploration 1/48 (about 2.08%), badge activities 1/32 (about 3.13%), and dungeon-clear partners 1/16 (6.25%). Every rarity has the same shiny chance within a source, including common Pokémon. This applies only to newly generated encounters, with no cumulative guarantee; existing partners are unchanged.

5. Defeating the final Forest dungeon boss and settling the rewards now reveals one capturable partner from the unlocked forest species. Find it in Encounters and catch it with a normal throw. The settlement screen announces the discovery. When five encounters are already waiting, the oldest is replaced. Failed saves can be retried and restarting does not duplicate the reward.

Back up before updating and keep the matching old installer. A full community installation may overwrite progress; imports still require the same device and matching firmware.
