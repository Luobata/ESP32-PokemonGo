2026.10.24 · A Letter from Johto

Continue beyond Kanto
100 second-generation partners expand the Pokédex to 251 species. The warehouse now holds 753 individuals, plus six party slots. Duplicate species remain independent; full storage requires a player-selected replacement and a second confirmation.
With any four Kanto badges, open Menu > Johto to receive an invitation, meet the professors and choose a new companion. Existing four-badge, eight-badge and post-Red saves retain their progress and can join the new chapter.

A connected investigation
An Adventure Journal points to the next destination, connecting bug observations, Unown ruins, rescues, the Burned Tower, Lake of Rage, Whirl expeditions and legendary investigations. Dialogues can pause and resume; replaying them does not repeat rewards.
17 fieldwork sites ask you to observe clues, choose a route and bring a suitable party member. A correct action costs five stamina; hints and wrong choices are free. Progress comes from solving the clue, not repeated random exploration. Specific solutions stay in collapsed sections of the online guide.

Routes, activities and battles
Eight Johto regions each offer three branches and enforce local level floors and ceilings. Togepi comes from its special egg. New activities include bug surveys, ice/whirl/tower expeditions, 26 Unown forms and partner rescues. Five new evolution items, cross-generation and branching evolution join the growth system; achievements expand to 43.
Eight new Gyms and badges are available. Defeating Red automatically enables champion teams with higher levels and distinct battle mechanics. Previously cleared normal Gyms can be rematched for separate champion achievements. Professor Elm's final trial includes legendary partners; the Lake of Rage story grants one guaranteed shiny red Gyarados.

Presentation and stability
Fixes cross-page rendering stalls and transition-band bounds, separates animation time from display rate, restores a more relaxed motion pace and improves audio switching. Fixes exploration blocked by an already-listed species and Wi-Fi log output interrupting USB import acknowledgements.

Updating safely
Update the firmware AND refresh the online save tool or download a new offline copy. Same-device V5–V31 backups are supported, with migration and V31 export/import round trips. Keep original files; newer saves cannot be restored into unsupported older firmware.
Back up first. Existing installations should use PokeWalk-update.zip over USB; upgrading from the 453-slot version keeps the partition layout. A full community installation can erase saves, so back up before installing and restore afterwards. Never overwrite the only copy of your progress.
