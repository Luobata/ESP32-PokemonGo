Turn AI Passport into a pocket Pokémon adventure. Meet Professor Oak, choose a Kanto starter or Pikachu, care for your partner and explore towards the Gyms, Elite Four, Champion and Red. After four Kanto badges, a letter from Johto opens the next chapter.

Collect 251 species, build a six-member party and keep up to 753 individuals in storage. Duplicate species remain separate, with their own growth, shininess and move preferences. Battles automatically choose from learned, enabled moves: no PP management or forced forgetting. Disable moves whose drawbacks do not suit your team.

Follow professors and Gym Leaders through bug surveys, Unown ruins, partner rescues and ice, whirl and tower expeditions. Bring your own team. The Adventure Journal points to the next step; 17 fieldwork sites ask you to read clues, choose a path and find a suitable helper. Optional answers stay behind collapsed sections in the online guide.

Earn Johto badges, hatch Togepi and investigate the Lake of Rage to receive a red Gyarados. After defeating Red, Johto Gyms gain champion teams and distinct battle mechanics. Professor Elm's legendary lineup and the forest's final echo await beyond them. Care, research and achievements connect collection with further adventures.

Pixel animation, scene music, volume/mute and brightness controls, and automatic screen-off support short daily sessions. Stamina refills in about an hour. Ambient exploration uses Wi-Fi environment changes, not real step counting. 194 moves are supported; this is not a complete recreation of the original games.
