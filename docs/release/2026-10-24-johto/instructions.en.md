Johto chapter
With any four Kanto badges, open Menu > Johto, read the invitation and choose a new companion. Existing progress is retained; new Johto badges are earned separately. Follow the Adventure Journal. C advances dialogue, A/B choose an answer and holding B pauses for later. Existing investigation progress and earned final-trial access remain valid.

Fieldwork and activities
Eight Johto regions each have three branches and local level floors/ceilings. Free exploration finds partners, supplies and research. Legendary evidence requires observing a site, choosing its path and a suitable living party member. Correct actions cost five stamina; hints and wrong choices are free. No specific species or move is required. Optional solutions are collapsed in the website guide.
After the first badge, collect the special egg and complete 12 valid Johto explorations to hatch Togepi. Two badges unlock a six-stop bug survey: bring one owned partner, pay 10 stamina and use your own balls, with up to three throws per candidate. Keep one candidate; finishing all six stops gives the story record even without a catch. The first two Goldenrod rescues, at six and twelve local research actions, provide unchosen starters.
Three badges open the first Unown wall. Five, six and seven badges unlock ice, whirl and tower expeditions: choose three owned partners, pay 20 stamina and bank rewards or go deeper at relays. Whirl's deep clear yields the Silver Wing. The lake story after five badges grants a guaranteed shiny red Gyarados; cancelled/full-storage claims remain pending.
After defeating Red, Johto Gyms automatically use champion levels and battle buffs, with separate first-clear achievements. Normal clears can be rematched. Follow the Journal for Elm's final trial prerequisites. New Johto achievement/research rewards retain eligibility if item storage is insufficient.

Getting started and controls
The game works offline. Follow Oak's introduction and choose Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle or Pikachu. Existing saves resume their progress. A moves up, B moves down, C confirms; hold B to return and C to turn off the screen. Idle screen-off occurs after about 60 seconds; the first wake press does not trigger a game action.
Choose Care, Menu or Encounters on the home screen. Menu contains Pokédex, Party, Items, Challenges, Options, Achievements, Exploration, Dungeons and Johto. Start with Exploration, choose a route and spend five stamina to find a partner. In capture, A/B change balls and C throws immediately. A failed direct capture allows one counterattack, then another choice; selecting Battle starts automatic combat. During trainer battles, C opens tactics; holding B requests escape or surrender confirmation.

Growth and storage
Feed berries, play when at least five stamina remains and confirm eligible evolution in Care. Party has six slots; warehouse has 753 independent individuals. Duplicates are allowed. At full storage, select a replacement after capture, confirm the displayed individuals and shiny warning; Cancel is the default. Hold A to change sorting. Hold B can discard the newly caught partner after confirmation. Keep power on until the storage decision is saved.
In Party or Warehouse, open individual details > Moves to toggle learned moves. At least one remains enabled; new moves start enabled. Settings follow that individual through swaps and evolution. Changes are unavailable during battles/dungeons. Warehouse > Details > Release works without moving the partner into the party; it requires confirmation and cannot be undone. Pokédex history stays. Hold A in the warehouse list for filters/sorting.

Stamina and rewards
Stamina caps at 100 and refills from zero in about one hour. Exploration and route trainers cost five; Gyms and Red cost ten; a full Elite Four/Champion run costs twenty once. Original dungeons cost twenty to start and use one to three owned partners; continuing is free. HP/status persist between nodes; banked rewards remain after defeat. Checkpoint/restoration and encounters are saved automatically.
Kanto exploration wins build a shiny chain for future encounters; page changes, restarts and other battle modes preserve it. Exploration defeat or confirming capture resets it. Capture during a chain asks again, defaulting to Cancel. Escape does not add or reset it. Old Kanto achievements can be claimed with full item stacks, discarding excess; new Johto achievements keep eligibility until there is room.

Settings and Wi-Fi time
Options controls mute, volume, brightness, names and battery display. For volume/brightness, C enters adjustment, A increases, B decreases and C finishes. Brightness ranges from 10% to 100%; volume changes do not unmute. Battery and cumulative screen-on playtime appear in Menu/Care. Settings persist.
For time sync, open Options > Wi-Fi Time and press C to start setup. Connect a phone to the PW- hotspot and password shown on the device, remaining connected despite the no-internet warning. Open http://192.168.4.1, enter an available 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network or a second device's hotspot and select Connect and Save. The device shows Time Synchronized on success. Setup closes after about five minutes; C toggles setup, holding B leaves it, and A outside setup retries sync. Correct details and retry on failure. Saved credentials reconnect at boot. First sync establishes the time reference; later boot sync credits powered-off time. Offline play remains available.

Backup, import and safe update
Use desktop Chrome/Edge at https://luobata.github.io/ESP32-PokemonGo/. Choose a private backup folder, connect USB, authorize the serial port and close competing serial tools. On the device open Options > Back Up Save and confirm. Wait for the website to write/read back the file and the device to finish. Files stay on your computer.
To restore, open Options > Import Save, choose a .pksave on the website and request import. The device defaults to Cancel; select overwrite and confirm. Current progress is backed up first; only then does import proceed. After restart reconnect and check the receipt, partners, levels and Pokédex. V5–V31 are supported on the same device; older saves migrate to V31 without editing files. New saves cannot be imported into unsupported old firmware. The developer restore.py command does not support expanded warehouse restoration; use the website.
Update firmware AND refresh the website or download a new offline tool. The new 72 KiB container needs the updated tool. A full community installation may erase progress: back up first and restore afterwards. Existing installations should use PokeWalk-update.zip at https://github.com/Luobata/ESP32-PokemonGo/releases/latest over USB, following its README. It checks the existing layout and backs up before writing; upgrading from the 453-slot version does not change partitions. If export fails, preserve existing backups and use the protected update package rather than erasing your only progress with full installation.
Offline: download the tool ZIP, unzip, run python3 server.py (Windows: py -3 server.py) with Python 3.8+ and open http://localhost:8767/. Keep original backups and complete error messages if an operation fails.
