Turn AI Passport into a pocket Pokémon adventure. Meet Professor Oak, choose a starter or Pikachu, and explore four starting areas plus eight regions unlocked by your progress, with the same first 151 Pokémon. Catch shinies, raise a party of six and earn evolution items through achievements. Train against twelve route opponents, then challenge Gyms, the Elite Four, the Champion and Red, or take your own partners into the Forest dungeon, eight themed dungeons and the Champion Expedition.

Battles automatically choose from learned moves, with no PP or forgetting. Pixel animation, scene music, volume, mute and screen sleep are included. Movement is inferred from Wi-Fi changes, not step counting. There are 191 moves; this is not a complete Gold/Silver recreation. This unofficial fan project credits artwork, music and naming sources in the repository.

Each badge unlocks another exploration activity. Rotating tracking targets, shinies and evolution items give you more to collect. Take your own partners through the Forest dungeon, combine upgrades and earn rewards along the way, and discover a capturable partner after clearing the final boss.

Menu and Care can show the battery level. Options includes a battery-display toggle, brightness, volume, mute and Wi-Fi time sync for powered-off stamina recovery. Online and offline USB tools back up saves and import them after device confirmation. You can import older saves on the same device when supported by the installed firmware. Back up before updating, and update both the device and save tool for cross-version import.

New regions offer three exploration directions, rotating targets, deeper investigation and research. Themes have different bosses, first-clear rewards and challenge difficulty. They share an eight-node structure; bespoke map puzzles and long story events are not included.

Each new region has three distinct habitats. Preview example partners and the encounter level range before choosing a trail. New-region encounter levels come from that region’s range; deeper exploration uses its upper half.
