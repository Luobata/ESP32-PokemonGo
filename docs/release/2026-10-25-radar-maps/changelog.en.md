2026.10.25 · Radar and Map Roaming

Two new ways to find partners
Menu now includes Radar and Roam. Radar senses nearby Wi-Fi without joining networks and uses the environment fingerprint to select a candidate pool. It does not provide real GPS coordinates, direction or distance.
Explore the park, Ruins of Alph and Burned Tower after two, three and four Johto badges respectively. Gold-style tiles, characters and walking frames bring trees, water, walls and NPCs into compact scenes with collisions and a following camera. These are three-button adaptations, not full original maps.

Walk, observe, then search
A/B selects a destination, C walks there, C again interacts, and holding B returns. Complete three free observations before searching at the fourth destination. Success adds one encounter and resets observations for the next circuit. Activity entrances connect to existing investigations. Successful searches also advance route research and carried-egg exploration; observation alone does not grant legendary evidence.
Both searches cost five stamina. Radar cools down for 120 seconds; each map independently cools down for 180 seconds. These use powered-on time, pause while switched off and survive reopening or restarting. A full five-encounter list blocks searching without replacing partners; failures do not charge stamina.

Higher shiny odds
Base odds are 1/32 (3.125%) for radar and 1/24 (about 4.17%) for roaming, independent of species rarity. Both add the existing exploration-chain bonus; ordinary exploration remains 1/48. At 20 wins, radar is about 3.96% and roaming 4.99%. Shininess is fixed when generated, not rerolled by changing pages.
Exploration victories grow the chain. Confirming capture or losing an exploration battle ends it, with a confirmation before capture. Other modes do not affect it; leaving, changing maps and restarting preserve it.

Guide and safe updating
A new guide section covers controls, unlocks, pools, levels and probability tables. Shiny and species pages are updated; the previous Johto guide remains selectable.
Upgrade firmware for these features. Same-device V5–V32 saves are supported; V5–V31 migrate while preserving partners and chapter progress. New observations and cooldowns start empty. Partitions and the backup container are unchanged. V31→V32 and V32→V32 overwrites were tested on hardware using a changed setting and readback. Refresh the guide; replace older offline tools when needed.
Back up first. Prefer the USB save-preserving update package for existing installations. Full community installation may erase saves, so restore afterwards. New saves cannot be imported into unsupported old firmware.
