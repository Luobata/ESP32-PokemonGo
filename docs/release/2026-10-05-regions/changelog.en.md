October 5: First-generation regions and themed expeditions

Explore eight new regions: Twin Islands, Ghost Tower, Safari Fields, Magnetic Mine, Volcanic Ruins, Dragon Valley, Abandoned Lab and Silver Snow Ridge. Badges, the Champion and regional research unlock them gradually. The original four routes remain, with the same first 151 Pokémon.

Each region has three exploration directions, rotating targets and independent research. Discover five local species to unlock deeper investigation. Rare partners are more common in the new pools; after five random discoveries below four stars, the next random discovery guarantees at least four stars. Legendary progression requirements still apply.

Eight themed dungeons and a Champion Expedition take 1–3 of your own partners through battles, upgrades and camps. Themes have distinct bosses, first-clear rewards and a harder mode. Enemy scaling is set on entry and does not chase level-ups during the run.

After clearing a new theme, choose a direction to find a capturable partner rated at least four stars. After four such clears without a five-star partner, the fifth guarantees one. Shiny odds are 1/16 for dungeon partners and 1/48 for exploration. First clears reward evolution items and Ultra Balls; repeat clears can also yield theme items.

If the encounter list or an item stack is full, new-theme rewards are held for collection. Collect them in the dungeon lobby, or discard them after a second confirmation, before starting another run. Regional research links discoveries, captures, tracking and a local clear with a one-time growth reward.

Also includes the previous pending community revision's legacy migration, verified restore results and save-preserving USB updater. V5–V17 saves migrate to V18; V18 can be exported and imported again. Existing Forest dungeon progress is retained. Keep original backups unchanged; V18 cannot be imported into old firmware.

Existing compatible devices can use PokeWalk-update.zip at https://github.com/Luobata/ESP32-PokemonGo/releases/latest and follow its USB instructions. Full community installation overwrites the old save area: back up before installation and import afterwards.
The new content requires a firmware update. The online/offline save tools fixed on October 5 already support V18; older tools must be refreshed or downloaded again.
